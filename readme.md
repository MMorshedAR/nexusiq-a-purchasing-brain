1. One-Line Pitch
NexusIQ is an AI-powered supply chain intelligence platform that helps businesses make smarter purchasing decisions, prevent stockouts, reduce excess inventory, and minimize supplier risks while optimizing limited budgets.

3. Public Summary
NexusIQ is an AI-powered inventory and supply chain decision-support platform designed for wholesalers, importers, distributors, retailers, and small manufacturers.

It transforms everyday inventory and purchasing data into actionable insights. By analyzing product demand, stock levels, supplier performance, lead times, and available budgets, NexusIQ helps businesses determine what to purchase, when to purchase it, and how much to order.

The platform provides demand forecasting, intelligent reorder recommendations, supplier risk analysis, purchasing budget optimization, and supply disruption simulations. It also explains the reasoning and estimated financial impact behind each recommendation, allowing businesses to make more informed and confident decisions.

NexusIQ aims to make intelligent supply chain management more accessible, especially for small and medium-sized businesses that cannot afford expensive enterprise-level solutions.

3. Problem Statement
Many wholesalers, importers, distributors, and small manufacturers still rely on spreadsheets, manual calculations, and basic inventory management systems to make purchasing decisions.

These methods often fail to provide accurate demand predictions, identify unreliable suppliers, or determine the optimal time and quantity for purchasing products.

As a result, businesses frequently experience:
- Stockouts: Essential products become unavailable because businesses fail to anticipate demand or supplier delays.
- Overstocking: Excess inventory ties up valuable working capital and increases storage costs.
- Supplier uncertainty: Businesses struggle to identify suppliers with unreliable delivery performance.
- Budget constraints: Limited purchasing budgets make it difficult to prioritize products effectively.
- Poor decision-making: Businesses lack clear insights into the financial consequences of different purchasing decisions.
Existing inventory systems may help businesses track what they have, but many lack the predictive and financial intelligence needed to determine what they should purchase next.

Consequently, businesses lose revenue, waste capital, and face unnecessary supply chain risks.
